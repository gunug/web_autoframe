Frequently asked

Where are my photos stored?

In your own Google Drive. AutoFrame is a frame that shows photos — it is not a backup service and does not take responsibility for storing them. Keep your originals elsewhere.

Does it play videos?

Yes. In the slideshow a video plays to the end before the next item, and you can toggle mute while it plays.

How is the age on a photo calculated?

It compares the person's date of birth with the photo's capture date to show the age at capture, alongside their age today. Events work the same way for elapsed time. People and events are a Premium feature.

What if my Drive fills up?

The usage gauge shows where you stand, and you can select and delete photos yourself. Keeping only the most recent N and threshold-based auto-clean are Premium features. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Is it safe to leave running all day?

There's a keep-screen-awake option, and the app explains how to exempt itself from battery optimization for long runs. Keeping it plugged in is recommended.

Which devices are supported?

Android phones, tablets and foldables. The grid adds columns automatically as the window gets wider.
